# Variable Relationship Notes

## Intended Correlations (Synthetic)
- Housing program enrollment → higher months_in_stable_housing (approx +3–5 months)
- Workforce program enrollment → higher employment rate and training hours
- Higher equity_barrier_index → lower service_utilization and lower wages
- Education level → modest positive association with starting wage
- Age → slight negative with training completion (older participants)

## Limitations
- Relationships are approximate, not causal
- No real administrative data
- Suitable for dashboard prototyping and methodology demonstration
- Not for policy inference without validation
